# USE: 'BE SUPPOSED TO', PAST EXPECTATION

**EGP #468** · **B1** · `egp-468`

## Can-do

Can use 'was supposed to', 'were supposed to' to refer to past or future arrangements which may not have happened or may change.

## Example

I am really sorry, but I will have to make some changes to my trip. I was supposed to arrive on 11th June 2003, but I will arrive the next month. (Mexico; B1 THRESHOLD; 2003; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
