# FORM: 'BE' EXPRESSIONS

**EGP #466** · **B1** · `egp-466`

## Can-do

Can use a limited range of expressions with 'be' + infinitive ('be allowed to', 'be supposed to', 'be able to') with present and past forms of 'be' and with modal 'will'.

## Example

First of all, if you are allowed to go out of the building in your break, you should do it. (Cyprus; B1 THRESHOLD; 2009; Greek; Pass)
