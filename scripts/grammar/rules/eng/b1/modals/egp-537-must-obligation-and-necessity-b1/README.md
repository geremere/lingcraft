# USE: OBLIGATION AND NECESSITY

**EGP #537** · **B1** · `egp-537`

## Can-do

Can use 'must' (with a wide range of pronouns and nouns) to talk about obligation and necessity.

## Example

[talking about something the speaker has found] She took it and said we must go and find him. (Poland; B1 THRESHOLD; 2008; Polish; Pass)
