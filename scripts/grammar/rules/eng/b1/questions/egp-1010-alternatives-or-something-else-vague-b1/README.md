# FORM/USE: 'OR SOMETHING ELSE', VAGUE

**EGP #1010** · **B1** · `egp-1010`

## Can-do

Can form alternative question with 'or something else' as the second alternative to a noun phrase, to refer to something non-specific.

## Example

Shall we buy him a book, a CD or something else? (Greece; B2 VANTAGE; 2000; Greek; Pass)
