# FORM: 'PLUS' WITH NOUNS

**EGP #264** · **B1** · `egp-264`

## Can-do

Can use 'plus' as coordinating conjunction, often in relation to numbers.

## Example

In my house we are six people, my mother, my father, my sister and my two brothers, plus my two cats, which are members of the family too. (B1 THRESHOLD; 2003; ; Pass)
