# FORM/USE: 'PLUS' WITH CLAUSES

**EGP #265** · **B1** · `egp-265`

## Can-do

Can use 'plus' to connect clauses and sentences, often to point out a positive addition or advantage.

## Example

I would be more than pleased to receive you at home, it's not too big, but we could have a lot of fun, plus you won't have to pay a thing! (Mexico; B1 THRESHOLD; 2004; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
