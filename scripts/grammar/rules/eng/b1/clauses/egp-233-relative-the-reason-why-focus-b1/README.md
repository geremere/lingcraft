# FORM/USE: 'THE REASON WHY', FOCUS

**EGP #233** · **B1** · `egp-233`

## Can-do

Can use defining relative clauses with 'why' after 'reason', to give an explanation and for focus.

## Example

The reason why I love it is that it's quite original because it's about a woman who can see ghosts. (Spain; B1 THRESHOLD; 2007; Spanish - European; Pass)
