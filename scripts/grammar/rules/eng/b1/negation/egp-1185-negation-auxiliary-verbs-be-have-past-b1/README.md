# FORM: AUXILIARY VERBS 'BE', 'HAVE', PAST

**EGP #1185** · **B1** · `egp-1185`

## Can-do

Can form negative statements of main verbs in the past continuous and past perfect with auxiliary verbs 'be' and 'have' + 'not/n't'. ► past continuous ► past perfect

## Example

It was raining for seventy two hours and it wasn't going to stop. (Ukraine; B1 THRESHOLD; 2008; Russian; Pass)
