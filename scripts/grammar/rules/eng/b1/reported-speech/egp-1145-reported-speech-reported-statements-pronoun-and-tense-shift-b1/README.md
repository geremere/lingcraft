# FORM: REPORTED STATEMENTS, PRONOUN AND TENSE SHIFT

**EGP #1145** · **B1** · `egp-1145`

## Can-do

Can report statements using a reporting clause with 'say' or 'tell' + 'that-'clause, with a pronoun and tense shift where relevant.

## Example

I immediately called her to see if everything was o.k. and she said that she had won the lottery and she wanted to give some of the money to me. (Italy; B1 THRESHOLD; 2008; Italian; Pass)
