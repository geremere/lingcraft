# USE: REPORTING VERBS

**EGP #881** · **B1** · `egp-881`

## Can-do

Can use the present simple with a limited range of reporting verbs, including 'say', 'show'.

## Example

For example, I know a person that moved to my city two years ago, and now he says that it improved his social life. (Preliminary English Test, B1, Italian)
