# USE: FIXED PLANS WITH 'WILL'

**EGP #392** · **B1** · `egp-392`

## Can-do

Can use 'will' to talk about fixed plans, often with timetabled times and dates.

## Example

The work will start at 8 a.m. and finish at 2 p.m. The new schedule starts on 1 June. (Russia; B1 THRESHOLD; 2007; Russian; Pass)
I won't be able to get there at the time we agreed. (Mexico; B1 THRESHOLD; 2008; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
