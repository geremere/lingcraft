# USE: 'BE GOING TO'

**EGP #366** · **B1** · `egp-366`

## Can-do

Can use the simple past form of 'be' + 'going to' to talk about the future from a point in the past. ► 'be going to'

## Example

She was going to return it but she saw something very strange, her number was on the screen. (Argentina; B1 THRESHOLD; 2008; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
