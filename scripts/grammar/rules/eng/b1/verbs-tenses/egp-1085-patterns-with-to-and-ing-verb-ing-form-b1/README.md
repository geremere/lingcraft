# FORM: VERB + '-ING' FORM

**EGP #1085** · **B1** · `egp-1085`

## Can-do

Can use a limited range of verbs followed by an '-ing' form.

## Example

I would like to stay there a few years to finish studying and to have my own money to start thinking of travelling. (Uruguay; B1 THRESHOLD; 2010; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
