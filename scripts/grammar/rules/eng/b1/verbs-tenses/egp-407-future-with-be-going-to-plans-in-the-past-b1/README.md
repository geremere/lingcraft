# USE: PLANS IN THE PAST

**EGP #407** · **B1** · `egp-407`

## Can-do

Can use the past form of 'be going to' (often followed by 'but') to talk about a plan, sometimes one that may have changed.

## Example

We were going to get married two months ago but we broke up and I really didn't know why. (Mexico; B1 THRESHOLD; 2008; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
