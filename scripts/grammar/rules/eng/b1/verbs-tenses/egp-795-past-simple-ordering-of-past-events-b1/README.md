# USE: ORDERING OF PAST EVENTS

**EGP #795** · **B1** · `egp-795`

## Can-do

Can use the past simple to order sequences of events in the past, in the context of narratives.

## Example

I remember her as a shy girl, but I read she became a manager, she went on lots of trips, she met a lot of new friends, she got married and then she got divorced. (Italy; B1 THRESHOLD; 2004; Italian; Pass)
