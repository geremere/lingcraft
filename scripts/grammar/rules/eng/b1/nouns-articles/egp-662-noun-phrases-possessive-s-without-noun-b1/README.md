# FORM/USE: POSSESSIVE '‘S' WITHOUT NOUN

**EGP #662** · **B1** · `egp-662`

## Can-do

Can omit the noun after possessive '’s' to talk about a place which is familiar to the speaker and listener.

## Example

Last week I left my clothes at the dry cleaner's and I can't collect them. (B1 THRESHOLD; 2009; Polish; Pass)
