# FORM/USE: 'THE OTHER'

**EGP #296** · **B1** · `egp-296`

## Can-do

Can use 'the other' with a singular noun to refer to the second or the opposite of two things, and with a plural noun to refer to one of a set of things. ► pronouns ►  noun phrases

## Example

She saw his blue jacket in a shop on the other side of the street. (Germany; B1 THRESHOLD; 2008; German; Pass)
