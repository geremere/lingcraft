# FORM: NOUN + 'OF' + POSSESSIVE PRONOUN

**EGP #663** · **B1** · `egp-663`

## Can-do

Can form noun phrases with noun + 'of' + possessive pronouns.

## Example

… we always did everything together and couldn't stop laughing as she told me many funny stories and I told her lots of silly mistakes of mine. (Vietnam; B1 THRESHOLD; 2009; Vietnamese; Pass)
