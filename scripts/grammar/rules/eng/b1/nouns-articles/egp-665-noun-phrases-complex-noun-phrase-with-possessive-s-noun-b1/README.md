# FORM: COMPLEX NOUN PHRASE WITH POSSESSIVE '‘S' + NOUN

**EGP #665** · **B1** · `egp-665`

## Can-do

Can form complex noun phrases with noun phrase + 'of' + noun phrase + possessive determiner ''s' + noun phrase.

## Example

I ran to the street and I saw the window of my neighbour's car was broken. (B1 THRESHOLD; 2007; Portuguese; Pass)
