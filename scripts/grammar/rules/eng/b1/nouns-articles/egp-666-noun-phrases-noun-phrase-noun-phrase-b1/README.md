# FORM: NOUN PHRASE, NOUN PHRASE

**EGP #666** · **B1** · `egp-666`

## Can-do

Can put two noun phrases together (in apposition) to refer to the same person or thing, usually separated by commas.

## Example

Mr Miller, the Sales Director, will visit us next Thursday. (Germany; B1 THRESHOLD; 2001; German; Pass)
