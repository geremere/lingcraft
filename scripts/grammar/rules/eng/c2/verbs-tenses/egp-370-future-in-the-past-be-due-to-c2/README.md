# USE: 'BE DUE TO'

**EGP #370** · **C2** · `egp-370`

## Can-do

Can use the simple past form of 'be' + 'due to' to talk about scheduled events in the future from a point in the past.

## Example

It was quite early in the morning, only 7.15 am, and my plane was due to leave in about 40 minutes. (Germany; C2 MASTERY; 1993; German; Pass)
