# USE: LONG-TERM INTENTIONS WITH 'SHALL'

**EGP #396** · **C2** · `egp-396`

## Can-do

Can use 'shall' to talk about long-term intentions.

## Example

I must believe, believe in myself and in everybody else, and mainly in what I look for, this way I shall never lose hope. (Mexico; C2 MASTERY; 1993; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
