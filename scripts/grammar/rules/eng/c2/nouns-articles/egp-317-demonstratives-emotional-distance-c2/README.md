# USE: EMOTIONAL DISTANCE

**EGP #317** · **C2** · `egp-317`

## Can-do

Can use 'that' and 'those' to convey emotional distance, often to express disapproval.

## Example

I also told her that if I had been in her shoes, I would not have put up with that sort of behaviour. (Greece; C2 MASTERY; 1993; Greek; Pass)
