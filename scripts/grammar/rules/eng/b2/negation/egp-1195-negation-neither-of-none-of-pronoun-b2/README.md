# FORM: 'NEITHER OF', 'NONE OF' + PRONOUN

**EGP #1195** · **B2** · `egp-1195`

## Can-do

Can use negative forms 'neither of' and 'none of' + pronoun or noun phrase with the affirmative form of the verb.

## Example

Neither of us is liable in this case. (Czech Republic; B2 VANTAGE; 2007; Slovak; Pass)
