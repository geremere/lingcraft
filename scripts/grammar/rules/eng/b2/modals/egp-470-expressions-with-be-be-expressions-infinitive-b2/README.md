# FORM: 'BE' EXPRESSIONS + INFINITIVE

**EGP #470** · **B2** · `egp-470`

## Can-do

Can use the full range of expressions with 'be' + infinitive ('be likely to', 'be due to', 'be meant to', 'be bound to', 'be sure to', 'be certain to', 'be obliged to') with present and past forms of 'be' and with modal 'will' .

## Example

People who read books are likely to be more knowledgeable. (Argentina; B2 VANTAGE; 2008; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
