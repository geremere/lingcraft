# USE: 'BE DUE TO', 'BE TO', SCHEDULES

**EGP #474** · **B2** · `egp-474`

## Can-do

Can use 'be due to' and, more formally, 'be to' to talk about things that are scheduled or expected.

## Example

I have attached the … company law directive enacted 2 years ago and which is due to be implemented by 2008. (Austria; B2 VANTAGE; 2007; German; Pass)
