# FORM: NOUN + 'OF' + POSSESSIVE DETERMINER + NOUN

**EGP #669** · **B2** · `egp-669`

## Can-do

Can form noun phrases with noun + 'of' + possessive determiner + noun + '‘s', particularly with 'friend' or 'friends'.

## Example

Actually, I know a great one and they can make a little discount for us because the owner is a friend of my father's. (Albania; B2 VANTAGE; 2009; Albanian; Pass)
