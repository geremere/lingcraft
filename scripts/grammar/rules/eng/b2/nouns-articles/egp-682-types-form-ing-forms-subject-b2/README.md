# FORM'-ING' FORMS, SUBJECT

**EGP #682** · **B2** · `egp-682`

## Can-do

Can use the '-ing' form of verbs as nouns in subject position.

## Example

Working until midnight every evening is not reasonable. Working late is not the solution and it is even dangerous. (France; B2 VANTAGE; 1993; French; Pass)
