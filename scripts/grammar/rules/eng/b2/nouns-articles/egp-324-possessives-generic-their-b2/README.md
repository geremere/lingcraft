# USE: GENERIC 'THEIR'

**EGP #324** · **B2** · `egp-324`

## Can-do

Can use 'their' before nouns to refer to a generic body or group of people expressed as a singular subject. ► generic pronouns

## Example

… yet everyone has their preference for one or another. (Greece; B2 VANTAGE; 2000; Greek; Pass)
