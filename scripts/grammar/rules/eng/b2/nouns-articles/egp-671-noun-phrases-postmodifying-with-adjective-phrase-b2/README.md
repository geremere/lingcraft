# FORM: POSTMODIFYING WITH ADJECTIVE PHRASE

**EGP #671** · **B2** · `egp-671`

## Can-do

Can form complex noun phrases by using adjective phrases as postmodifiers to give more information about the noun.

## Example

Besides, we could leave one day in the morning and go by boat to a small and exotic island full of different and exotic species of birds that we could observe and photograph. (Turkey; B2 VANTAGE; 1993; Portuguese; Pass)
