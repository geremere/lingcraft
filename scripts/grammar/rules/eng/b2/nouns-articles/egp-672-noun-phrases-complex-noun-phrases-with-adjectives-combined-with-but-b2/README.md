# FORM: COMPLEX NOUN PHRASES WITH ADJECTIVES COMBINED WITH 'BUT'

**EGP #672** · **B2** · `egp-672`

## Can-do

Can form an increasing range of complex noun phrases with more than one adjective combined with 'but'.

## Example

I am very lucky, because I was born in a small but beautiful city called Neuva Helvecia. (Uruguay; B2 VANTAGE; 2003; Spanish - Latin American; Pass)
