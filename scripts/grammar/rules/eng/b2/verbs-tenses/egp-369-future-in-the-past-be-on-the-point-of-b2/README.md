# USE: 'BE ON THE POINT OF'

**EGP #369** · **B2** · `egp-369`

## Can-do

Can use the simple past form of 'be' + 'on the point of' + '-ing' to talk about things that were expected to happen soon after a point in the past.

## Example

In addition, when I was on the point of leaving the office, another phone rang. (Greece; B2 VANTAGE; 2002; Greek; Pass)
