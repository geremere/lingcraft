# FORM: VERB + NEW SUBJECT + '-ING' FORM

**EGP #1090** · **B2** · `egp-1090`

## Can-do

Can introduce a new subject before the '-ing' form (using noun or object pronoun).

## Example

If your boss doesn't mind you delaying, it could be a possible solution. (Germany; B2 VANTAGE; 1993; German; Pass)
