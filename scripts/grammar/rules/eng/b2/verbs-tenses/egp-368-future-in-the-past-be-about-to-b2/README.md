# USE: 'BE ABOUT TO'

**EGP #368** · **B2** · `egp-368`

## Can-do

Can use the simple past form of 'be' + 'about to' (often with 'just') to talk about the immediate future from a point in the past.

## Example

Two days before, I told him that our father was about to buy a brand new car. (France; B2 VANTAGE; 2000; French; Pass)
