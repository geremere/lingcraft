# FORM/USE: REPORTING VERBS, PAST CONTINUOUS

**EGP #1158** · **B2** · `egp-1158`

## Can-do

Can use the past continuous of the reporting verb to report mental processes, with a finite or non-finite 'wh-'clause in the reported clause.

## Example

I was wondering why I failed. (Greece; B2 VANTAGE; 1993; Greek; Pass)
