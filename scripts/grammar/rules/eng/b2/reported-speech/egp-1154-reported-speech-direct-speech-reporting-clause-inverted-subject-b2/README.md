# FORM: DIRECT SPEECH, REPORTING CLAUSE, INVERTED SUBJECT

**EGP #1154** · **B2** · `egp-1154`

## Can-do

Can report speech directly inverting the subject and verb in the reporting clause where the subject is a proper noun or noun phrase.

## Example

"I was astonished when I saw them standing there", said John. (Netherlands; B2 VANTAGE; 1999; Dutch; Pass)
