# USE: POSSIBILITY IN THE PAST

**EGP #524** · **C1** · `egp-524`

## Can-do

Can use 'might not have' + '-ed' to talk about possibility with a past reference.

## Example

The survey, mentioned in the local newspaper yesterday, might not have asked an accurate sample of people. (Brazil; C1 EFFECTIVE OPERATIONAL PROFICIENCY; 1999; Portuguese; Pass)
