# FORM/USE: HEDGING

**EGP #484** · **C1** · `egp-484`

## Can-do

Can use 'be' + 'not' + adjective + 'that-' clause to make an assertion less direct.

## Example

[talking about a town near a dump where people cannot open their windows] I am not certain that they have got used to it. (France; C1 EFFECTIVE OPERATIONAL PROFICIENCY; 1993; French; Pass)
