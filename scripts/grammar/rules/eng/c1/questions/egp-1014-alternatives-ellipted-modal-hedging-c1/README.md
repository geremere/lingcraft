# FORM/USE: ELLIPTED MODAL, HEDGING

**EGP #1014** · **C1** · `egp-1014`

## Can-do

Can form alternative questions with two clauses and ellipsis in the second clause, often as a hedging device.

## Example

Maybe you could hand out toys or show a movie? (Sweden; C1 EFFECTIVE OPERATIONAL PROFICIENCY; 2005; Swedish; Pass)
