# FORM: WITH ADVERBS

**EGP #410** · **C1** · `egp-410`

## Can-do

Can use 'be' + 'going to' with an increasing range of adverbs (particularly adverbs of certainty) in the normal mid position.

## Example

Mario Soares is already an old man, nearly 70, who is probably going to finish his active political life in 2 or 3 years. (Portugal; C1 EFFECTIVE OPERATIONAL PROFICIENCY; 1993; Portuguese; Pass)
