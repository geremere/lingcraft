# USE: POLITENESS: 'I THOUGHT'

**EGP #807** · **C1** · `egp-807`

## Can-do

Can use the past simple 'I thought' as a politeness structure to sound less direct.

## Example

I understand you are quite concerned about the matter and I thought it would be a good idea to let you know what is really going on. (C1, Spanish ? Latin American)
