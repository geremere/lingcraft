# FORM/USE: 'WHATSOEVER', EMPHASIS

**EGP #1201** · **C1** · `egp-1201`

## Can-do

Can use 'whatsoever' after a negative form for emphasis.

## Example

This means that nothing shall change for the employees, there shall be no implication whatsoever for their rights stipulated in their employment contracts. (Czech Republic; C1 EFFECTIVE OPERATIONAL PROFICIENCY; 2007; Czech; Pass)
